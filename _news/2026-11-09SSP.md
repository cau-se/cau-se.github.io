### Our Contributions to SSP 2026

Our Contributions to the [17<sup>th</sup> Symposium on Software Performance (SSP 2026)](https://www.performance-symposium.org/ssp-2026/), which takes place in Würzburg from November 9<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026:

- **Overhead Comparison of OpenTelemetry and Kieker in Python** <br>
  David Georg Reichelt (Lancaster University Leipzig), Shinhyung Yang (Kiel University), Wilhelm Hasselbring (Kiel University)
- **The Time Measurement Pattern in the JVM** <br>
  David Georg Reichelt (Lancaster University Leipzig), Shinhyung Yang (Kiel University), Wilhelm Hasselbring (Kiel University)
- **Ecoscape v2: Declarative Performance Resilience Benchmarking in the Edge-Cloud Continuum** <br>
  Hendrik Reiter (Kiel University), Wilhelm Hasselbring (Kiel University)
- **Explaining the Write Performance of Kafka under Hardware-Related Chaos Scenarios** <br>
  Mika Petersen (CAU Kiel), Hendrik Reiter (CAU Kiel), Wilhelm Hasselbring (CAU Kiel)
- **Bringing PlantUML to Kieker: Streamline the Visualization Pipeline Toward Kieker-Jupyter Notebook Interoperability** <br>
  Yorrick Josuttis (Kiel University), Shinhyung Yang (Kiel University), Wilhelm Hasselbring (Kiel University)
- **Analysis and Visualization of Kieker’s Software Evolution** <br>
  Malte Hansen (Kiel University), Daniel Riese (Kiel University), Johannes Klindt (Kiel University), Wilhelm Hasselbring (Kiel University)
