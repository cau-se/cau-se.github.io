### Our Contributions to IRSC 2026 and RSECon26 in Sheffield, UK

The First International Research Software Conference [IRSC 2026](https://www.researchsoft.org/irsc/ and the 10<sup>th</sup> Anniversary Edition Research Software Engineering Conference [RSECon26](https://rsecon26.society-rse.org/) take place from 7<sup>th</sup> - 11<sup>th</sup> September 2026 in Sheffield, UK, with the following contributions from our group:

- Birds of a Feather: [Research Software Metascience](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g4kd0ifb0GmRybssyVWEHRGKbqyHW8JOv317X-lzfvk/)
- [David Georg Reichelt, Shinhyung Yang, Marcel Hansson, Wilhelm Hasselbring: **MooBench: A micro-benchmark for performance overhead measurement of observability tools**](https://virtual.oxfordabstracts.com/event/76908/submission/54)
