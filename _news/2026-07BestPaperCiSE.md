### 2025 Best Paper Award Runner-Up in Computing in Science & Engineering

We are honored that our paper entitled "Better Architecture, Better Software, Better Research" has been selected as the Runner-Up of the 2025 Best Paper Award, for Computing in Science & Engineering by the IEEE Computer Society Publications Board.

The list of winners and runners-up is posted online and can be found here: [2025 Best Paper Award Winners](https://www.computer.org/publications/2025-best-paper-award-winners).
